OUR STORY

Built around one problem: inconsistent sterile supply.

Dispoline India was founded to solve a recurring problem faced by Indian hospitals — inconsistent quality and availability of single-use sterile disposables. Over 20+ years, we've built a controlled-environment manufacturing operation that produces custom, surgery-specific procedure kits alongside a full range of gowns, drapes and barrier systems.

Every product is manufactured under a certified Quality Management System for Medical Devices, using globally sourced medical fabrics, with full batch-to-batch traceability from raw material to hospital delivery.

Quality is non-negotiable

Every product that leaves our facility passes through a validated, documented quality process.
